Ron Swanson Quotes Slack Bot

Click the button below to add the bot to your Slack workspace. First you'll be redirected to Slack where you'll authorize the bot, then you'll be redirected back to your team's Slack.

Usage

Type /ron or /swanson to get a random Ron Swanson quote. You can optionally add a topic or keyword to match on (for example /ron bacon, /ron yoga, or /ron canvas):
